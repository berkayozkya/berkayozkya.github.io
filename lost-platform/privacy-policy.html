Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Android and iOS game 03:17 - The Lost Platform (application identifier com.berkay.kayipperon). Contact: berkay.ozkaya.yedek@gmail.com.

Game data and purchases

The game does not require an account, name, email address or social sign-in. Story progress, the diamond balance, daily reward progress and offer timing are stored on your device. These saves are not a cloud-synchronised wallet.

Apple or Google processes payments. The game does not receive your payment card number. RevenueCat verifies purchases and restores permanent ad removal. It processes an app-generated user identifier, store transaction and product identifiers, purchase history and purchase status for functionality and purchase analytics. Purchase processing uses encrypted network connections.

Advertising and consent

Google AdMob provides banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads. Depending on your platform, consent and device settings, its SDK can collect and share IP addresses (including inferred approximate location), advertising or other device identifiers, app/ad interactions and diagnostic information. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Its SDK encrypts data in transit. The game does not request precise GPS location, contacts, microphone access or your photo library.

The game requests consent where required using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can revisit required consent choices through the game's privacy choices control. Device privacy settings also control advertising identifiers. Permanent ad removal removes banner and interstitial ads; rewarded ads remain optional.

Service providers, retention and your choices

Store and SDK providers process data under their own policies and may process it outside your country. Local saves remain on the device until removed through device app-data controls or uninstallation, subject to operating-system backup behaviour. Purchase records may be retained to verify transactions, restore entitlements and meet store or legal requirements.

For access, correction or deletion requests, contact the email above and identify the game and relevant purchase or installation. We may need information to verify that the request concerns your data. Deleting a local save does not automatically delete store or RevenueCat records, cancel purchases or issue a refund. Store refunds and provider-specific data controls are managed by their respective providers. Do not send payment card details.

The game is intended for players aged 16 and above and is not directed at children. Updates to this policy will be dated on this page.

Delete your Trick Shot account

Deletion permanently removes your cloud account, level progress, lives, gems, Aim Assist balance and game transaction records. It also requests deletion of your RevenueCat customer record. Apple and Google keep their own store purchase history; deleting an account does not refund purchases.

Sign in with the same Google or Apple account you use in Trick Shot.

Another way to request deletion

You can also delete your account in the game: Settings → Cloud Account → Delete Account. If you cannot sign in or do not have the game installed, email a deletion request to berkay.ozkaya.yedek@gmail.com from your account email. We may ask for proof of account ownership; do not send a password. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days.

Apple users: after deletion, remove Trick Shot in Apple Account → Sign in with Apple.

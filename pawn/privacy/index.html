Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Pawn Shop Boss handles information when you use the game.

Information We Collect

Pawn Shop Boss does not require an account and does not operate its own user database. Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

Third-Party Services

The game may use third-party services for ads, in-app purchases, and purchase validation. These services may process device, purchase, and diagnostic information under their own privacy policies.

  • Apple App Store for purchases and platform services
  • Google AdMob for advertisements
  • RevenueCat for purchase management

Local Game Data

Progress, settings, and gameplay state are saved locally so the game can continue where you left off. Removing the app may delete this local data.

Children

Pawn Shop Boss is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, open a request at GitHub Issues.

Common Issues

  • If a purchase does not appear, restart the app and check your App Store purchase history.
  • If rewarded ads are unavailable, wait a few minutes and try again. Ad availability can vary by region.
  • If the game layout looks wrong, update to the latest version and restart your device.

Languages

The game supports English and Turkish. You can change language in the in-game settings menu.
